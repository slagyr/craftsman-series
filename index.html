Robert C. Martin • 2002-2010

The Craftsman Series

A long-form software engineering narrative by Robert C. Martin (Uncle Bob), now organized as a browsable static site. The series follows an apprentice programmer through refactoring, testing, design pressure, team dynamics, architecture, and later Clean Code essays.

What This Is

The series is written as a continuing story rather than a conventional textbook. It mixes narrative, code listings, refactoring steps, acceptance tests, brown-bag discussions, and later craft essays.

What Survived

The articles are normalized Markdown reading copies converted from the original documents. Where possible, diagrams and local code archives are linked directly inside the episodes.

Reading Order

The intended path is numerical order, from episode 1 through episode 63. The arcs below group the story into its major phases to make browsing easier.
